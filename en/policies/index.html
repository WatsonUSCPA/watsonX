1. Customer Support & Contact

For questions about our services, quotes, engagements in progress, or to request a refund or cancellation, please reach us through any of the following:

  • Contact form (preferred): use the contact form on the Fee Estimator page.
  • Direct message on X (Twitter): @watsonuscpa
  • Existing clients: simply reply to the email thread used for your engagement.

We reply within 2 business days (Japan time). Replies may take longer during filing season (January–April and September–October).

2. Refund & Dispute Resolution Policy

2-1. Basic principle — services provided under an engagement letter

Our services (tax return preparation, tax consultations, and related work) are professional services provided under an engagement letter signed before work begins. Scope of work, fees, payment terms, and refund terms are governed by the engagement letter. If this page and your engagement letter differ, the engagement letter controls.

Amounts shown by the fee estimator are approximate minimum fees. No fee is charged until you agree to a firm written quote.

2-2. Refunds

  • Before work begins: no fee is due. Any prepayment already made is refunded in full.
  • After work begins, before delivery: if you cancel, we bill only for the work completed up to your cancellation (a pro-rated settlement based on progress) and refund the remainder. We provide a written breakdown of the settlement by email.
  • After delivery: given the nature of professional services, refunds after delivery are generally not available. However, if there is an error attributable to us (such as a preparation mistake on a return), we will correct it — including preparing an amended return — at no charge.
  • Method and timing: refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days of the settlement amount being finalized.

2-3. Dispute resolution

  • If you have questions or concerns about our services, fees, or deliverables, please contact customer support first. We will review the matter and work with you in good faith to resolve it.
  • If you are considering a chargeback through your card issuer, we ask that you contact us first to avoid misunderstandings — most matters are resolved quickly through direct discussion.
  • Governing law and venue for disputes that cannot be resolved by discussion are set out in the engagement letter.

3. Cancellation Policy

3-1. Subscriptions and recurring billing

We do not offer subscription or recurring-billing services. There is no auto-renewal and no recurring automatic charge of any kind. Every engagement is a standalone contract under its own engagement letter.

3-2. Cancelling a tax preparation engagement

  • Before work begins: you may cancel at any time, free of charge. No reason is required.
  • After work begins: we settle for the work completed up to the cancellation and refund any remainder (see the refund policy).

3-3. Booking, rescheduling, or cancelling a consultation (hourly)

  • To book a consultation, first send us your consultation topic from the Fee Estimator page; we then guide you to our booking calendar to request a time. Your booking is a request and is confirmed once we approve it. You may withdraw a request at any time before approval, at no charge.
  • After confirmation, we send an invoice by email for the consultation fee.
  • Once confirmed, you may still reschedule or cancel free of charge until the business day before your appointment.
  • Same-day cancellations and no-shows may be charged up to one hour of the consultation fee for the reserved time.

4. Terms of Service & Restrictions

4-1. What we provide and how

  • We provide professional services relating to US taxation — return preparation and consultations. The specific scope of each engagement is defined in its engagement letter.
  • Deliverables and correspondence are provided electronically (email, etc.). We do not sell or ship physical goods, so no shipping restrictions or physical-goods return rules apply.
  • No import or export of goods is involved, so no export-control restrictions apply to our services. Clients residing in either the United States or Japan are welcome.

4-2. Informational content and tools — disclaimer

  • The articles, decision tools, and fee estimator on this site are provided for general information only and do not constitute tax or legal advice for your specific situation.
  • Tax rules and thresholds change by year and by individual circumstances. Personalized advice is provided as an engagement under an engagement letter.
  • Input to the tools and the estimator is processed entirely in your browser and is not sent to us unless you explicitly submit a form.

4-3. Fees and promotional terms

  • Amounts shown by the fee estimator are approximate minimum fees. Your firm quote is confirmed by email after we review your situation, and takes effect when you agree. If additional fees become necessary after work begins, we explain them and obtain your agreement before proceeding.
  • When discounts or promotions are offered, their conditions, eligibility, and expiration are stated at the time of the offer. Discounts cannot be combined unless expressly stated.

4-4. Your information

  • Information you send through our forms is used only to respond to your inquiry, prepare your quote, and communicate about your engagement. It is never shared with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.
  • Please do not include sensitive information such as your SSN (Social Security number) or account numbers in the forms. Sensitive information needed for an engagement is collected separately through secure means after the engagement begins.

4-5. Changes to this page

We may revise this page to reflect changes in law or in our services. Material changes are announced on this page, with the date of the latest update shown at the top.
