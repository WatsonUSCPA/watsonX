Refunds, Cancellations & Terms

Engagement letter

All services (tax return preparation, consultations) are professional services provided under an engagement letter signed before work begins. Scope, fees, payment terms, and refund terms are governed by the engagement letter; if this page and your engagement letter differ, the engagement letter controls. No fee is charged until you agree to a firm written quote.

Refunds & disputes

  • Before work begins: no fee is due, and any prepayment is refunded in full.
  • After work begins, before delivery: if you cancel, we bill only for the work completed to date (with a written breakdown) and refund the remainder.
  • After delivery: given the nature of professional services, completed work is generally non-refundable. However, if an error on our part is found (e.g. a preparation mistake), we will correct it — including preparing an amended return — at no charge.
  • How refunds are issued: to the original payment method, within 10 business days of the amount being finalized.
  • Disputes: if you are unhappy with any aspect of our service, please contact us first — we will work with you in good faith to resolve the issue, and most matters are settled quickly this way. We ask that you reach out to us before initiating a chargeback. Governing law and venue for unresolved disputes are set out in the engagement letter.

Cancellations

  • We do not offer subscriptions or recurring billing. Every engagement is a one-time contract — there is nothing that renews or auto-charges.
  • You may cancel an engagement free of charge any time before work begins. After work begins, the refund terms above apply.
  • Hourly consultations can be rescheduled or cancelled free of charge until the business day before the appointment. Same-day or no-show cancellations may be charged up to one hour of the consultation fee.

Terms & restrictions

  • All deliverables and correspondence are provided electronically (email, etc.). We sell no physical goods, so no shipping — and no shipping or export restrictions — apply. Clients residing in either the US or Japan are welcome.
  • The articles, tools, and this estimator are general information only and do not constitute tax or legal advice for your specific situation. Rules and thresholds change by year and by facts; personalized advice is provided under an engagement letter.
  • Estimator results are minimum fees. Your firm quote is confirmed by email; if additional fees become necessary after work begins, we explain them and obtain your agreement before proceeding.
  • When promotions or discounts are offered, their conditions and expiration are stated at the time of the offer; discounts cannot be combined unless expressly stated.
